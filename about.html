✳ THE VISION

A regional meeting of minds. A healthier future.

The concept note outlines a platform for practical and responsible AI innovation across healthcare.

✳ WHY SAHIX

Reimaging Healthcare: AI Without Borders

AI is opening new possibilities in diagnosis, medical imaging, public health, medical research and health service delivery. Southern Africa has an opportunity to advance these tools in ways that respond to local priorities.

SAHIX is designed to encourage collaboration between clinicians, researchers, government, universities and technology companies — and to inspire young innovators to tackle regional challenges.

“From new ideas to meaningful impact: healthcare innovation should benefit communities across the region.SAHIX conference vision
