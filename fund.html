✳ RURAL DIGITAL HEALTH FUND

A healthier digital future for every community.

The proposed Sponsor a Clinic programme aims to improve access to health technologies in underserved Zimbabwean facilities.

✳ FROM IDEAS TO IMPACT

Support the clinics that need it most.

The Rural Digital Health Fund is a proposed initiative to mobilise organisations and individuals around practical health technology support for rural and marginalised facilities.

A planned Digital Health Kit may include a computer, internet equipment and initial connectivity support, paired with training where needed.
