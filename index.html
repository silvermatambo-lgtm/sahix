✳ WELCOME TO SAHIX

Where healthcare meets the power of AI

The AI in Health Conference 2027 aims to bring together healthcare professionals, researchers, universities, policymakers, innovators and industry partners across Southern Africa.

The vision is practical: connect ideas, share knowledge, showcase emerging technology and develop partnerships that strengthen healthcare systems.
